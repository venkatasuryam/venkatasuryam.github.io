WHEN LIFE FEELS HEAVY

Strain at work or at home.
You need not carry it alone.

Pressure at work, conflict with an employer or colleagues, strain in a marriage or family, or a long stretch of worry can affect sleep, confidence and peace of mind. Many people find comfort in speaking with someone patient, and in the steadiness of traditional spiritual practice.

What a consultation can offer

  • An unhurried conversation in Telugu, Hindi or English, without judgement.
  • A traditional astrological perspective on the period you are going through, for reflection.
  • Optional spiritual practices, such as prayer, mantra, meditation or simple daily routines, that you may choose to follow.
  • Encouragement towards practical steps: rest, honest communication, and support from family, friends and professionals.

If you are facing harassment or feel unsafe at work or at home, support from HR, legal advice or the authorities matters too.

What it is not

This is not diagnosis, therapy or treatment, and it does not replace a psychiatrist, psychologist or counsellor. Please continue any prescribed medication or therapy. Where it seems helpful, he will encourage you to seek professional support as well.

PLEASE REACH OUT FOR PROFESSIONAL HELP

If distress lasts more than two weeks, disturbs sleep, appetite, work or relationships, or if you have any thoughts of harming yourself, please speak with a doctor or mental-health professional.

BEFORE YOU BEGIN

A few common
questions.

What is muhurtam?+

Muhurtam is the traditional selection of an auspicious time for an important occasion. Guidance can consider the nature of your occasion, its location and relevant personal details.

What should I prepare for a consultation?+

Start with your main question. For horoscope guidance, birth date, time and place may be requested. For a ceremony, have your preferred dates and location ready. Confirm the details needed when you enquire; there is no need to send personal documents in an initial message.

Can I enquire from outside India?+

Yes. The practice has helped people in India and abroad. An initial discussion can take place by phone. Please enquire about availability, time-zone arrangements and options for meeting in person.

Can astrology help with stress, anxiety or depression?+

Speaking with someone patient and following traditional spiritual practices can bring comfort and a perspective for reflection. They are not a treatment for anxiety, depression or any other mental-health condition. If you are struggling, please also speak with a doctor or counsellor, or call Tele-MANAS on 14416 (free, 24 × 7).

Can a particular outcome be promised?+

No particular outcome can be promised. Vedic astrology is a traditional, belief-based practice that can offer a perspective for reflection. Your circumstances, choices and practical action remain important.

How does spiritual guidance fit with professional advice?+

Spiritual practices can accompany practical support. Health, mental-health and fertility concerns should be discussed with qualified medical professionals; financial, legal and relationship concerns may also need appropriate professional advice.
