BEFORE YOU BEGIN

A few common
questions.

What should I prepare for a consultation?+

Think about the main question or problem you would like help with, and since when it has been troubling you. Keep your date, time and place of birth ready; if the birth time is not known exactly, that is fine — just say so in the form. For muhurtam, have the occasion, place and preferred dates ready.

How do I pay, and is my payment safe?+

Pay the ₹1,116 consultation fee by UPI (PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm or any UPI app) directly to his UPI ID shown on this page. The payment happens inside your own UPI app; this website never sees your bank or card details. Note the 12-digit UPI reference (UTR) and enter it in the consultation form.

Can I book from outside India?+

Yes. The practice has helped people in India and abroad, and the WhatsApp call works anywhere. If you cannot pay by UPI from outside India, please send a WhatsApp message to arrange another way to pay. Booking times are shown in your own time zone.

Can astrology help with stress, anxiety or depression?+

Speaking with someone patient and following traditional spiritual practices can bring comfort and a perspective for reflection. They are not a treatment for anxiety, depression or any other mental-health condition. If you are struggling, please also speak with a doctor or counsellor, or call Tele-MANAS on 14416 (free, 24 × 7).

Can a particular outcome be promised?+

No particular outcome can be promised. Vedic astrology is a traditional, belief-based practice that can offer a perspective for reflection. Your circumstances, choices and practical action remain important.

How does spiritual guidance fit with professional advice?+

Spiritual practices can accompany practical support. Health, mental-health and fertility concerns should be discussed with qualified medical professionals; financial, legal and relationship concerns may also need appropriate professional advice.
